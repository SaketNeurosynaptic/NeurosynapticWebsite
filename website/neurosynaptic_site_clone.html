ReMeDi® Platform

The ReMeDi® Platform is a cloud-based telemedicine application that connects doctors, nurses, and administrators across multiple ecosystems. It supports seamless consultations, diagnostics, and prescriptions, with offline functionality for areas lacking connectivity, ensuring efficient healthcare delivery in remote regions.
  • Cloud-Based Telemedicine Application
  • Point-of-Care Diagnostics
  • ABDM Compliant and eSanjeevani Ready

Central Medical Facility Primary screening and consultation to the patients remotely; management of the entire healthcare ecosystem

  • Multi-Platform Integration
  • Offline Mode Capability
  • Comprehensive Management Information System (MIS)

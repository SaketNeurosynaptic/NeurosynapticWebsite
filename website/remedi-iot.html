Overview

What is ReMeDi IoT?

ReMeDi IoT is a connected point-of-care diagnostics solution. Your platform sends a test request; a health worker performs the tests with ReMeDi medical devices; results — structured readings, PDF reports, images and audio — flow back to your server automatically. No manual re-entry, no extra server at the facility.

The gap between ordering a test and getting the result

  • Tests are ordered in one system but performed on standalone devices that don't talk to it.
  • Readings are copied by hand, delaying care and inviting transcription errors.
  • ECG traces, auscultation audio and images rarely reach the patient record in their original form.

Three parts. One connected workflow.

01

ReMeDi medical devices

Bluetooth and USB point-of-care devices from the portable ReMeDi Kit — the same devices already used in the field.

02

ReMeDi Android app

Runs on a tablet or phone. The health worker sees pending test requests, taps Take Test and captures readings from connected devices.

03

ReMeDi Cloud

Securely routes each request to the right health worker and pushes results back to your platform.

How it works

Six steps from test order to result in your system.

  1. Step 1

    Authenticate

    Your platform signs in with issued credentials and receives a time-bound access token.

  2. Step 2

    Send a test request

    Share patient details, tests required, the health worker's phone number and your own visit ID.

  3. Step 3

    Routed to the health worker

    ReMeDi Cloud places the request on the health worker's pending list.

  4. Step 4

    Perform the tests

    The health worker taps Take Test and uses the connected ReMeDi devices.

  5. Step 5

    Upload results

    Readings and reports — PDF, images and audio — are uploaded from the app.

  6. Step 6

    Delivered to your server

    ReMeDi Cloud securely pushes results and files back to you, retrying until you acknowledge receipt.

Frequently asked questions

Which platforms can integrate with ReMeDi IoT?

Any health platform — State HMIS, hospital HIS/EMR or third-party telemedicine — that can send test requests and receive results.

Do we need a server at each facility?

No. An Android tablet or phone, the ReMeDi Kit and internet are enough.

How is this different from ReMeDi e-Sanjeevani?

The app UI and devices are the same; only the routing in ReMeDi Cloud differs, sending results to your platform.

In what format do results arrive?

Structured readings, PDF reports (ECG, spirometry, dermatoscope, otoscope), images and WAV stethoscope audio.

What if our server is temporarily unavailable?

ReMeDi Cloud retries the result push until your server acknowledges it.

How do we get started?

We issue client credentials via the admin portal and share a Postman collection, integration guide and self-test harness.

Partnerships

At Neurosynaptic, our mission to revolutionize healthcare delivery is made possible through the collective strength of our partnerships. Over the years, we have collaborated with a diverse range of organizations, including governments, CSR donors, NGOs, healthcare providers, and technology innovators, to create scalable, impactful solutions.

From global foundations and leading corporations to grassroots organizations, each partnership reflects our shared commitment to improving access to quality healthcare, empowering underserved communities, and driving innovation. Together, we’re bridging the healthcare gap for millions.

Here are some of the valued organizations we’ve worked with:

IPE Global’s USAID-backed Samriddh
Successful projects under IPE Global’s USAID-backed Samriddh program demonstrate Neurosynaptic’s leadership in sustainable healthcare solutions in rural India.
ACT Grants, HCL Foundation, NSE Foundation, Bayer Foundation
The company has been awarded grants and support from esteemed foundations, validating its significant impact in global healthcare development.
Grand Challenges Canada – Stars in Global Health
Recognized under Grand Challenges Canada’s Stars in Global Health for its impactful work improving rural healthcare access through technology.
Invested in by IAN, HealthQuad, KOIS
Strong investors like IAN, HealthQuad, and KOIS highlight NCPL’s trustworthiness, innovation, and sustainability in healthcare technology.
Millennium Alliance, SPARSH program under DBT
Neurosynaptic has earned significant recognition for its contribution to health innovation under programs like Millennium Alliance and SPARSH.
Global Health Labs Joint Development Collaboration
NCPL’s collaboration with Global Health Labs, a premier research organisation, exemplifies its role as a leader in global healthcare innovation.

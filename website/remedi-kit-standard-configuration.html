ReMeDi Kit – Standard Configuration

The standard ReMeDi® Kit contains following devices:

BLOOD PRESSURE

PORTABLE DIGITAL BLOOD PRESSURE METER: ReMeDi® Blood Pressure Monitor (NSCPL/R/B)

  • Ability to operate to automatically inflate and deflate.
  • Systolic pressure, diastolic pressure and pulse rate display capability.
  • With measurement cuff for adult and pediatric patients, with size between 13 and 52 cm.
  • Capacity for pressure reading:
  • Systolic 60 mm Hg to 230 mm Hg.
  • Diastolic 40 mm Hg to 180 mm Hg 
  • Pulse measurement of 40 bpm to 240 bpm.
  • Rechargeable internal battery, with charge indicator. 
  • Ability to operate and synchronize data online and offline.
  • Ability to start and detect reading from the application interface of the software directly.
  • Communication via Bluetooth, with PC or laptop or portable devices.
  • Automatic transfer of data to the software application in real time and direct entry to the patient’s electronic telemedicine medical record.
  • Weight of 454 grams, including batteries.
ELECTROCARDIOGRAM

PORTABLE DIGITAL ELECTROCARDIOGRAM: ReMeDi® Electrocardiogram (NSCPL/R/C)

  • Acquisition of twelve (12) simultaneous leads.
  • Trade online and offline.
  • Start and reading detection from software application interface directly.
  • Communication via Bluetooth, with PC or laptop or portable devices.
  • Lead detection.
  • Baseline stabilization.
  • Filters to eliminate power line interference and muscle tremor.
  • Heart rate calculation.
  • Frequency response from 0.05 Hz to 150 Hz.
  • Automatic transfer of data to the software application in real time and direct entry to the patient’s electronic telemedicine medical record.
  • Rechargeable internal battery, with charge indicator. 
  • Weight of 150 grams, including batteries.
  • Protection: defibrillation.
  • Sensitivity: 1 bits/sample; or 10 mm/mV or less.
FETAL DOPPLER

PORTABLE FETAL DOPPLER:

  • Ultrasonic probe:
  • Working mode: Continuous Doppler
  • Waterproof: IPX1
  • Acoustic-working frequency: 2.5MHz ±15%
  • Ultrasonic output power: ≦ 10 mW/ cm2 
  • Volume control.
  • Capability of fetal heart rate:
  • Measurement range: 50 ~ 210bpm
  • Accuracy: ±2bpm 
  • Electromagnetic compatibility: Class I Type B.

Physical Characteristics:

  • Size: 80 mm (Diagram)*43 mm (height))
  • Weight: 75g ± 10g (include battery)
  • Communication via Bluetooth, with PC or laptop or portable devices
  • Ability to start and detect reading from the software application interface directly.
  • Automatic transfer of data to the software application in real time and direct entry to the patient’s electronic telemedicine medical record.
  • Rechargeable internal battery, with charge indicator.
  • Ability to operate and synchronize data online and offline.
GENERAL EXAMINATION CAMERA

GENERAL EXAMINATION CAMERA FOR TELEMEDICINE.

  • Uses: Telemedicine, interoral exams, dermatological exams, micro and macro approach options, patient education.
  • Sensor resolution 2592 x 1944.
  • Native optical magnification: 15x ±50x and digital: 15x ±150x.
  • Lens Assembly: 3-layer glass, 650nm cutoff.
  • Integrated polarizer with variable settings.
  • Video format: MJPG.
  • Frame rate: up to 30 FPS.
  • Video/image properties: Color: hue, saturation.
  • Exposure: Brightness, contrast.
  • Image: Sharpness, gamma.
  • Video, image files: JPG/BMP/AVI.
  • Lighting: 16 ultra-bright LEDs four (4) brightness settings.
  • Interface: USB2.0.
  • Dimensions: 17.7 cm long 5 cm in diameter (nose cone) 3.5 cm in diameter (body)
  • Communication via USB, with PC or laptop or portable devices
  • Power USB 5VDC, 150mA, powered directly from the console.
  • Automatic transfer of data to the software application in real time and direct entry to   the patient’s electronic telemedicine medical record.
  • Ability to operate and synchronize data online and offline.
  • Ability to start and detect reading from the software application interface directly.

Implementation Engineer (2 positions)

Role Overview

Implementation Engineers will be part of the core execution team responsible for deploying and operationalizing NCPL’s technology platforms across projects. This role sits at the intersection of technology, operations, and client engagement — requiring close coordination with internal teams and active involvement in client-facing implementation. The focus is on ensuring that systems are not just delivered, but successfully adopted and sustained in the field. This role is suited for individuals who enjoy hands-on execution, working across teams, and solving real-world implementation challenges. It requires comfort with both technology and field-level coordination, along with a strong sense of ownership.

Responsibilities

Implementation & Deployment
  • Support the rollout of NCPL platforms and projects across multiple project locations 
  • Assist in system configuration, deployment readiness, and go-live activities 
  • Work closely with Development and QA teams to ensure releases are implementation-ready 
  • Participate in User Acceptance Testing (UAT) and ensure timely issue resolution 
Coordination & Execution
  • Act as a link between internal teams (Development, QA, Product) and on-ground implementation teams 
  • Coordinate with stakeholders, partners and field teams to ensure smooth execution of deployment plans 
  • Track progress against timelines and ensure closure of implementation milestones 
Requirement Understanding & Gap Analysis
  • Engage with clients to understand operational requirements and workflows 
  • Document requirements and support translation into system configurations or enhancements 
  • Identify gaps between client needs and existing platform capabilities and flag appropriately 
Issue Management & Support
  • Manage daily support tickets, ensuring prioritization and timely resolution 
  • Track bugs, enhancements, and change requests across projects 
  • Support risk identification and mitigation to avoid delays or disruptions 
Project Tracking & Reviews
  • Participate in regular project review and status meetings 
  • Highlight blockers, dependencies, and risks in a timely manner 
  • Contribute to improving processes for faster and more efficient implementation 
Training & User Enablement
  • Conduct training sessions for client teams, operators, and field staff 
  • Support onboarding of new users and locations 
  • Ensure effective system usage and adoption through continuous engagement 
Post-Implementation Support
  • Provide ongoing support after go-live to ensure system stability 
  • Gather user feedback and share inputs for improvements 
  • Ensure a smooth transition from implementation to steady-state operations

Qualifications & Experience

  • Bachelor’s degree in Engineering, Biomedical, Electronics, IT or related fields 
  • Diploma in Computer/Electronics / Electrical /IIHT/Jetking, CCNA certification preferred
  • 2–5 years of experience in implementation, technical support, or project coordination roles 
  • Experience working with cross-functional teams such as Development, QA, and Product 
  • Exposure to UAT, client onboarding, and implementation lifecycle processes 
  • Experience handling ticketing systems and managing issue resolution workflows

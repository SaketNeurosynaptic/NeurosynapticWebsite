ReMeDi Solutions Suite

The ReMeDi® Solutions Suite:
The Heart of Comprehensive Healthcare

ReMeDi® by Neurosynaptic is a groundbreaking suite of telehealth, e-health, and m-health solutions designed to redefine healthcare delivery. Purpose-built for screening, primary diagnosis, and triaging, ReMeDi® connects the entire healthcare ecosystem—from medicines and diagnostics to doctors, village health centers, and specialists.

At the center of our approach lies the ReMeDi® Solutions Suite, developing solutions first, then leveraging the right tools and technologies to execute. Our solution suite leverages a combination of advanced technologies and lower dependency on skilled medical personnel, making affordable and effective healthcare delivery a reality at scale. At Neurosynaptic, we provide flexible, customizable solutions that meet the unique needs of our partners and programs worldwide.

The Solutions Suite blends our ReMeDi® Platform and Kits with bespoke services, building tailored ecosystems that deliver long-lasting, real-world impact. Through our Solutions Suite, NCPL works collaboratively with partners to build flexible, scalable healthcare delivery systems, ensuring:

Customizable implementation models, adaptable across geographies, demographics, and operational needs.

Integration of new technologies and third-party diagnostic tools for enhanced program capabilities.

Alignment with local regulations and compliance, including support for national digital health frameworks like ABDM and e-Sanjeevani.

Scalable solutions that drive impact, from grassroots outreach to urban health innovations.

ReMeDi® Kit

The ReMeDi® Kit is a portable, compact diagnostic powerhouse designed to deliver high-quality healthcare services at the point of care. Packed in a lightweight, 3.5 kg briefcase, this kit integrates over 40 diagnostic tests ranging from physiology (ECG, blood pressure, SPO2, etc.) to infectious diseases (malaria, dengue, HIV) and non-communicable conditions like diabetes and lipids. It also addresses maternal and child health needs with features like fetal Doppler and hemoglobin monitoring.

Key Features

  • Indigenous Technology
    Entirely designed, developed, and manufactured in India under government-supported programs like BIRAC.
  • Wireless and Energy Efficient
    Our wireless devices are designed for optimal energy efficiency, ensuring extended battery life for seamless field operations. This makes them ideal for remote and resource-constrained settings, enabling uninterrupted healthcare delivery over extended periods.
  • Safe-Devices Technology
    Patent-pending system ensures accountability by linking device usage directly to the healthcare network, minimizing errors and preventing misuse.
  • Robust and Rugged
    Tested in over 2,500 rural centers across India and 10 other countries, the kit is built to handle demanding conditions.
  • Ease of Use
    A single-button interface enables minimally trained operators to deliver accurate results.
  • Expanding Diagnostic Capabilities
    ReMeDi® integrates seamlessly with third-party devices, expanding its range of diagnostic offerings with the latest technologies. This includes AI-based diagnostic support, enabling more accurate interpretation of results and enhancing decision-making for healthcare providers.
By integrating seamlessly with the ReMeDi® Platform, the kit empowers grassroots healthcare providers to deliver accurate diagnostics and real-time data capture, making it the ideal solution for last-mile healthcare delivery.

ReMeDi® Platform

The ReMeDi® Platform is a cutting-edge, cloud-based telemedicine application that supports the seamless delivery of end-to-end healthcare services. Its flexibility allows for easy integration of new diagnostic devices and technologies, ensuring adaptability to evolving healthcare needs. The platform is designed to align with regional and global compliance requirements, offering customization for specific projects, languages, and country-specific workflows. This adaptability makes it a truly versatile solution for diverse healthcare ecosystems worldwide.

Key Features

  • Point-of-Care Diagnostic

    Supports a wide range of tests, including physiology, urine, blood, and maternal health diagnostics, integrated into the platform for real-time data entry and analysis.

  • Complete Workflow Automation

    Enables patient registration, appointment scheduling, video consultations, diagnostics, prescriptions, and referrals—all in one platform.

  • Multiple Ecosystem Integrations

    Compatible with both Windows-based PCs and Android smartphones, ensuring flexibility and wide adoption.

  • Management Information System (MIS)

    Offers comprehensive reporting and analytics on patients, tests, prescriptions, and overall operational performance.

  • ABDM and e-Sanjeevani Compliant

    Fully aligned with India’s Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission and e-Sanjeevani guidelines, making it ready for government deployments.

  • Advanced Features

    Includes multi-party video conferencing, remote training capabilities, and an admin module for customizable configurations.

  • Offline Functionality

    Designed for low-connectivity areas, the platform stores data locally and syncs with the cloud when connectivity is restored.
  • The ReMeDi® Platform has powered over 2500 rural health centers across India and supported operations in 10 countries, demonstrating its robustness and scalability for diverse healthcare needs.

Why Choose ReMeDi®?

With its unique combination of indigenous technology, third-party integrations, and AI-driven diagnostics, ReMeDi® is transforming the healthcare landscape. Whether it’s providing life-saving diagnostics in underserved rural areas or streamlining workflows in urban hospitals, the ReMeDi® Solutions Suite delivers high-quality, scalable, and cost-effective healthcare where it’s needed most.
